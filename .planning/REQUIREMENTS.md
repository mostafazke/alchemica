## Candidate Features (from Future Requirements in v2)

These were explicitly scoped out of v2 and are candidates for v3:

### Monetization
- **MOTZ-01**: Rewarded video ad grants one free hint (AdMob via Capacitor native wrapper)
- **MOTZ-02**: Remove-ads one-time IAP ($2.99–$4.99)
- **MOTZ-03**: Hint bundle IAP — purchase 10 hints

### Social
- **SOCL-01**: Cloud save synced across devices (Google/Apple sign-in)
- **SOCL-02**: Async leaderboard — compare discovery count with friends
- **SOCL-03**: Global leaderboard (top discovery counts)

### Platform
- **PLAT-01**: Capacitor native wrapper for iOS and Android
- **PLAT-02**: Google Play Games Services integration

### Daily Engagement
- **DALY-06**: Push notification when daily challenge resets
- **STRK-05**: Streak bonus — higher combo multiplier during active streak

### Content
- **CMS-01**: In-app editor UI — add/edit/delete elements and reactions without code changes
