Alchemica

Design Direction Overview
Six variations within the warm-stone-brass-glass material metaphor established in brainstorming. Each explores a different thermal balance — from barely-warm to ember-hot.
  • A: Subtle Warmth — The Step 8 reference palette. Neutral-warm, maximum versatility. The brass is present but doesn't dominate.
  • B: Deep Brass — Leans into the brass metaphor. Everything is slightly more golden. The workshop is lit by candlelight.
  • C: Stone & Glass — Cooler stone (slightly purple-gray undertone). The glass metaphor leads; brass plays supporting role. Most "mineral" feeling.
  • D: Ember Workshop — Orange-warm undertone. The forge is active. More saturated, more energetic. The most emotionally intense direction.
  • E: Moonlit Brass — Cool-neutral base with brass accents. Night laboratory. The brass glows against near-gray darkness. Highest contrast between base and accent.
  • F: Warm Parchment — Lightest backgrounds of the set. More parchment-like. The text is the warmest. Feels most like an illuminated manuscript.

Evaluation Criteria

Material authenticity — Does it feel like a physical space? Stone, brass, glass — not digital chrome.

Element legibility — Can you read 61+ element icons on the shelf grid without strain?

Emotional warmth — Does it invite lingering, or does it feel sterile?

Discovery moment — When the overlay appears, does the background recede enough for the new element to feel special?

OLED friendliness — True black areas for power savings. No large mid-gray panels.

Accessibility — Text contrast ratios, touch target visibility, color-blind safety.
